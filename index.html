About Chicken AI

Chicken AI is a web-based and mobile-friendly application designed to detect poultry diseases from images of chicken droppings. It leverages deep learning and image classification to assist farmers in identifying diseases early.

Key Features

  • 📷 Upload real chicken droppings images directly from your phone or camera
  • 🧠 Deep learning model trained on 6000+ real-world poultry images
  • 💡 Supports blurry and low-light images using intelligent enhancement filters
  • 📊 High-accuracy prediction (98%) for diseases like Salmonella, Coccidiosis, and Newcastle
