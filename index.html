About Chicken AI

Chicken AI is a web-based and mobile-friendly application designed to detect poultry diseases from images of chicken droppings. It leverages deep learning and image classification to assist farmers in identifying diseases early.

Key Features

  • 📷 Upload or capture chicken feces images
  • 🧠 AI-based disease prediction (Salmonella, Coccidiosis, Newcastle, etc.)
  • 📡 Works offline after setup
  • 📊 Achieved 98% accuracy using DenseNet on 6000+ images
  • ⚙️ Clean, responsive UI for mobile and desktop
